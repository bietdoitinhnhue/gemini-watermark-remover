FAQ

Useful answers, up front.

Does the video tool support both 16:9 and 9:16?+

Yes. The workspace handles supported landscape and portrait videos, including common 16:9 and 9:16 formats.

Which video formats can I select?+

You can choose MP4, WebM, or MOV files. Playback and export compatibility can still vary by browser and source codec.

Is my video uploaded to an app server?+

No. Detection and processing run in the browser on the device that opened this page.

Will every video have the same result?+

Results depend on the source video, visible mark, frame quality, and browser capabilities. Review the output before using it.
